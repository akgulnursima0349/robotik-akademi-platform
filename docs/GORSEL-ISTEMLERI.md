# İsteğe bağlı kapak görselleri

Portal kartlarındaki Blok Atölyesi ve Robotik Laboratuvarı kapakları şu an kodla çiziliyor; internetsiz ve hafif. Junior ve ortaokul kapaklarıyla aynı üslupta görsel istersen Gemini'ye şu istemleri ver.

- **Boyut:** 1600×1000 (16:10).
- **Biçim:** webp.
- **Dosya adları:** `apps/portal/src/assets/kapak-blok.webp` ve `kapak-lab.webp`.

Görseli bana verdiğinde kartlara bağlarım.

## Blok Atölyesi

> Flat vector illustration, soft pastel colors, white background with light warm tint. A friendly teal robot with a rounded square head, screen face with two dark oval eyes and a small smile, a red ball antenna, standing on a top-down garden grid made of sand-colored tiles and green trees. Big rounded coding blocks in orange, blue and purple float on the left like puzzle pieces with simple arrow icons (no text). Orange carrots on some tiles. Children's educational style, clean lines, no text, no letters, 16:10.

## Robotik Laboratuvarı

> Isometric flat vector illustration, snowy winter neighborhood in a Turkish city at dusk, pastel houses with snow-covered roofs and warm windows. A small blue robot snowplow with a yellow blade clears a dark asphalt path through white snow, snow particles flying to the sides. Soft blue-grey background, gentle snowfall, clean geometric style, no text, no letters, 16:10.
